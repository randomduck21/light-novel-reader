The rain had stopped by the time the town lights appeared beyond the hill. Kapi pulled his coat tighter and looked down at the road, wondering how many chapters the night still had left.

A warm light flickered in one of the distant windows. It was small, ordinary, and somehow reassuring. He kept walking, listening to the quiet rhythm of his footsteps.

At the bridge, he stopped for a moment. The river carried reflections of the lamps downstream, breaking them apart and joining them together again.

Tomorrow would bring another page, another problem, and probably another long evening at the desk. For now, though, the road was quiet.
